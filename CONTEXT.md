# LuminaIQ Learning Context

LuminaIQ turns a learner's study documents into connected study material and guided learning experiences.

## Language

**Topic**:
A named subject extracted from one or more Study Documents and available for learning activities.

**Topic Graph**:
The persisted set of prerequisite and related relationships among Topics in a Project.
_Avoid_: Knowledge graph, mindmap

**Graph Build**:
The generation of Topic relationships from the current Topics in a Project.
_Avoid_: Knowledge graph when referring to the generation process

**Graph View**:
The learner-facing projection of Study Documents, Topics, and their relationships.
_Avoid_: Graph Build, Topic Graph

**Learning Path**:
An ordered study sequence derived from Topic prerequisites and the learner's selected targets.

**Study Document**:
A learner-provided or imported source whose extracted content supplies Topics and study material.
_Avoid_: Book when referring to any uploaded source
